Play Console answers

Prepared 9 August 2026 from a read of the source, not from memory. Every "no" below is a claim about the code.

Answer the Data Safety form from this page rather than from impression. A declaration that does not match app behaviour is one of the faster ways to get an app suspended, and it is checked against the binary.

One answer depends on a decision that is still open. "Is all user data encrypted in transit" is Yes only if the backend is served over https. The release build already refuses to build against a plain-http backend, so this holds as long as the hosting decision produces an https origin. If it somehow does not, this answer changes and so does the privacy policy.

Why approximate and not precise, and why declared at all. What leaves the device is the latitude and longitude of the map area on screen, sent to Open-Meteo to get a forecast for it. That is a viewport, not a device fix, which is why approximate is the honest box. But if the user pressed the locate button, the map is centred on them, so the region being requested is their region. Declaring nothing would be defensible on a strict reading and wrong in spirit, and the spirit is what gets audited.

2. Permissions

Three are declared, and a store listing has to justify each one.

PermissionJustification to give
INTERNET The app is a live map. Radar, alerts, observations and imagery are all fetched.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION To move the map to the user's region on request and show what the weather is doing around them. A county is the smallest unit most of this data has.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION The accuracy circle drawn on the map is only honest if the fix is. A 3 km circle over a tornado warning is the difference between "us" and "not us".

Neither location permission is required to launch, and android:required="false" on the GPS feature keeps the app installable on a device without one. There is no background location permission, so the prominent-disclosure and background-access review path does not apply.

5. Store description

Both fit inside Play's limits and neither contains an em dash. Every claim below was checked against the code on 9 August 2026: the layer count is 38 unique data-layer inputs in index.html, of which exactly three (countries, states, radar) carry checked. Short description (65 of 80 characters)

Live weather radar, NWS alerts, aircraft and ships on a 3D globe.

Full description (2,213 of 4,000 characters)

Graticule is a live map of Earth. It opens on a 3D globe and draws what is happening right now, layer by layer, with nothing switched on that you did not ask for.

WEATHER
Radar on a timeline you can scrub, so you can see which way a cell is moving instead of guessing from one frame, plus a short forecast loop where the source provides one. National Weather Service watches, warnings and advisories, drawn as the actual warning polygon rather than a shaded county. Storm Prediction Center outlooks, local storm reports and spotter positions. Surface observations from METAR stations, with a selectable field. Forecast fields from Open-Meteo with an hour selector. Air quality, cloud cover, aurora, and a day and night terminator that tracks real time.

THE SKY
Aircraft from ADS-B with altitude, heading and type. Satellites propagated from public orbital elements. Airports, temporary flight restrictions, and FAA Class Airspace.

THE SEA AND THE GROUND
Ships from AIS. Tide stations, buoys and river gauges. Earthquakes, volcanoes, wildfires, hurricanes and tsunami notices. Country, state and county boundaries, cities, undersea cables, and US parcel outlines where they are published.

HOW IT BEHAVES
No account. No ads. No analytics library, no crash reporter and no advertising ID anywhere in the app, which is not a promise but a test that runs on every change.

Your location is used on the device to move the map to you and draw your accuracy circle. It is not transmitted and it is not stored.

Boundary and coastline data ships inside the app, so the globe still draws with no signal. The live feeds need a connection.

Thirty-eight layers, and every one is off until you turn it on, apart from radar and the boundary lines. Two of them, 3D buildings and photorealistic 3D tiles, need a free key of your own.

Graticule is free. There is no paid tier and nothing to unlock.

WHAT IT IS NOT
This is a situational awareness map, not a navigation or aviation instrument. The airspace and airport layers are drawn from public FAA data and are not maintained for flight planning. Nothing here replaces an official National Weather Service alert or an instruction from your local emergency management.

7. Hosting

Decided and half done. The two listing URLs are live now. The backend is written and configured but not deployed, because deploying it needs an account and a card.

PieceWhereCostState
Privacy and support pages https://graticule.vercel.app Free Live, verified public with no login wall
The app's backend Fly.io, one machine, graticule.fly.dev $6.64/mo plus $0.02/GB out Configured, never deployed

Why Fly and not something cheaper or free. The backend cannot be serverless: its feed loops are long-lived asyncio tasks and the state they build is in memory, so a platform that scales to zero stops the feeds, and one that scales out multiplies the polling against donated ADS-B infrastructure. That leaves a small always-on machine. Fly gives one machine with automatic TLS and first-class websockets for about $6.64/mo; a Hetzner VM is roughly $2 cheaper and adds operating system patching, TLS renewal and firewall upkeep, which is a bad trade on an app with no users yet.

The machine is 1 GB because the process was measured at 356 MB resident with every feed loaded, and serialising the unbounded snapshot briefly costs several hundred more. 512 MB would be tight.

Egress is worth one line. At the old 35 MB boot snapshot, a thousand app launches a day would have been about a terabyte a month, which is roughly $20 of bandwidth. After the deferred-snapshot change it is about 3.4 MB a launch, so the same traffic is nearer $2. The fix pays for the machine several times over.
