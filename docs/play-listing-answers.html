Play Console answers

Prepared 9 August 2026 from a read of the source, not from memory. Every "no" below is a claim about the code.

Answer the Data Safety form from this page rather than from impression. A declaration that does not match app behaviour is one of the faster ways to get an app suspended, and it is checked against the binary.

One answer depends on a decision that is still open. "Is all user data encrypted in transit" is Yes only if the backend is served over https. The release build already refuses to build against a plain-http backend, so this holds as long as the hosting decision produces an https origin. If it somehow does not, this answer changes and so does the privacy policy.

1. Data Safety

Top-level questions

QuestionAnswer
Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? Yes, one: approximate location. See below for why it is approximate and not precise.
Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Yes, conditional on https hosting. See the note above.
Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? No, and the reason is that nothing is retained to delete. There is no account and no server-side record of a user. Settings live in the device's local storage and are removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling.

Why approximate and not precise, and why declared at all. What leaves the device is the latitude and longitude of the map area on screen, sent to Open-Meteo to get a forecast for it. That is a viewport, not a device fix, which is why approximate is the honest box. But if the user pressed the locate button, the map is centred on them, so the region being requested is their region. Declaring nothing would be defensible on a strict reading and wrong in spirit, and the spirit is what gets audited.

2. Permissions

Three are declared, and a store listing has to justify each one.

PermissionJustification to give
INTERNET The app is a live map. Radar, alerts, observations and imagery are all fetched.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION To move the map to the user's region on request and show what the weather is doing around them. A county is the smallest unit most of this data has.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION The accuracy circle drawn on the map is only honest if the fix is. A 3 km circle over a tornado warning is the difference between "us" and "not us".

Neither location permission is required to launch, and android:required="false" on the GPS feature keeps the app installable on a device without one. There is no background location permission, so the prominent-disclosure and background-access review path does not apply.
