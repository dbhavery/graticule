Graticule

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 August 2026. Last updated 8 August 2026.

Graticule has no account, no sign in, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information about you, because we never receive any.

That is the whole policy in one sentence. The rest of this page exists because "we do not collect anything" is easy to write and hard to check, so below is the specific detail: what stays on your device, what the app sends over the network, and who receives it.

Location

Location access is optional, and the app works without it. Graticule asks for it only when you press the locate button, or when you have turned on "start at my location" in Settings. If you decline, the locate button tells you it is off and every other feature behaves normally.

When you grant it, the coordinate is used entirely on your device, for two things: moving the map camera to where you are, and drawing the blue accuracy circle around that point. The coordinate is not sent to our server, not written to storage, and not retained. It exists in memory until you close the app.

The one thing worth understanding: after the camera moves, forecast data for the map area you are now viewing is requested from Open-Meteo, the same as for any other place you navigate to. Open-Meteo receives the coordinates of that map view. It does not receive your device's location fix, and it is not told that the view is centred on you, but if you used the locate button then the area being requested is your area. That is stated plainly here rather than buried, because it is the only case where using this app puts anything near your position onto the network.

What is stored on your device

Graticule keeps your preferences in your browser's local storage, on the device only. Four keys:

  • graticule.settings.v1, your Settings choices, such as units, which layers are on, and the theme
  • graticule.tab, the panel tab you had open
  • graticule.rail, whether the side rail is collapsed
  • graticule.division, your chosen map division

None of it identifies you and none of it is transmitted. The app also caches map assets so it can open without a connection. Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling it, removes all of it. There is no server side copy to delete.

What the app sends over the network

Every request made by any app or website reveals your IP address to whoever receives it. That is how the internet routes a reply, and no app can avoid it. What an app can control is how many parties receive one. Graticule routes almost all of its weather data through a single backend, which fetches from the National Weather Service, USGS, NOAA and the rest on your behalf, so those agencies never see your device at all.

These are the parties your device contacts directly:

ReceivesWhat it gets, and why
Graticule backend Requests for shared feeds: alerts, observations, buoys, rivers, tides, spotter reports, cameras. These are the same for every user and carry no parameters about you. The live layer connection is receive only: the app never sends a message on it. The server runs with its HTTP access log switched off.
Open-Meteo Latitude and longitude of the map area you are viewing, to return a forecast for it. See the note under Location above.
Map and imagery providers
(Esri ArcGIS, OpenStreetMap, NASA GIBS, Iowa State Mesonet)		 The tile coordinates of what is on your screen, which is how any map loads. No identifiers are attached.
Asset hosts
(Cesium, jsDelivr, flagcdn, GitHub)		 Requests for the 3D globe engine, a satellite orbit library, country flags and an aircraft model. Static files, identical for everyone.
Google Fonts A request for the two typefaces the interface uses. This page and the support page do not use it.

Two further services, Cesium ion and Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles, are contacted only if whoever runs the backend has configured optional API keys for them. The default build has neither and contacts neither.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is read
  • No analytics, telemetry or usage measurement of any kind
  • No crash or error reporting service
  • No third party tracking SDK is present in the app
  • No profile is built about you, because there is no identifier to build one against
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected

Children

Graticule is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data all depend on a company holding some. We hold none about you. If you want everything Graticule has put on your device gone, clear the app's storage or uninstall it, and it is gone. If you believe otherwise, write to the address below and we will look into it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who receives it, the updated date at the top will change and the app will link to the new version. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes rather than applied silently.
