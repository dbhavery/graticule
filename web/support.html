Last updated 8 August 2026.

Graticule is a live weather and situational map: a 3D globe carrying radar, National Weather Service alerts, surface observations, river gauges, tides, buoys, earthquakes, fires, storm reports and aircraft, updating as they update.

Before anything else

Graticule is not an official source of warnings and must not be used to make a life safety decision. It displays data published by the National Weather Service and other agencies, and it can be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable because of a network problem at either end.

For a warning that affects you, the authority is the National Weather Service at weather.gov, your local emergency management agency, and NOAA Weather Radio. Follow their instructions, not this map.

Common problems

The locate button does nothing, or says location is off

Graticule needs location permission to move the map to where you are. Open your device Settings, then Apps, then Graticule, then Permissions, and allow Location. Nothing else in the app depends on it. If permission is already on and the fix still fails, the device may not have one yet: go somewhere with a clearer view of the sky and try again.

The app takes a while to open

It does, and we are working on it. The first launch builds a 3D globe and pulls live data for the whole world, which is heavy. Later launches are faster because the map assets are cached. If it never finishes, see the next item.

The globe is black, or no data appears

The map imagery and the live feeds both need a working connection. Check that you have one, then close the app fully and reopen it. If the globe draws but the layers stay empty, the data backend is unreachable, which is a problem on our end rather than yours. Please report it.

The back button does not close the app

That is deliberate. Back closes whatever panel, sheet or search you have open, one at a time. Once nothing is open, the app tells you that pressing back again will exit, and pressing it again does exit.

A layer is missing, or the map looks empty

Layers can be switched off individually. Open the layers panel and check that the one you want is enabled. Some layers only carry data over the United States, and some only show anything when there is something to show, for example hurricanes out of season.

My settings were lost

Settings live only on your device, so clearing the app's storage or reinstalling it resets them. There is no cloud copy to restore, by design. See the privacy policy.

Privacy

Graticule has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and your location never leaves your device. The privacy policy lists specifically what is stored and which services receive a request.

Where the data comes from

Graticule displays public data produced by these organisations. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any of them.

Airspace is drawn for context and is not navigation data. It comes from the FAA's published Class Airspace, simplified for display, and the Class E en-route blanket is not drawn at all. Aeronautical charts and the FAA's NASR subscription are the authoritative products. Do not plan a flight from this map.
